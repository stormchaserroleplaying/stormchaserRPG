# Goblin
*Small Humanoid (Goblinoid), Neutral Evil*

### Defences
**STR:** 9, **DEX:** 12, **CON:** 10, **INT:** 10, **WIS:** 9, **CHA:** 9<br>
+1 [parry](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Armour/Glossary/#parry) bonus vs. melee attacks (Scimitar, Buckler)

**Stamina:** 20 (d6 Recovery Die)

**Damage Reduction:** 1, +1 vs. <img src="https://img.icons8.com/ios-filled/12/FFFFFF/sword.png"> (Leather Armour)

### Ability Scores
**STR:** 8 (-1), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 10 (+0), **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 8 (-1), **CHA:** 8 (-1)

**Speed:** 22 feet

### Actions
**Scimitar:** *Melee Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 1d6+2 slashing damage.

**Self Shortbow:** *Ranged Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, ([strength](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#strength) 7, [two-handed](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/WeaponTableGlossary/#two-handed)), range 90 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 1d6+2 piercing damage ([ammunition](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/WeaponTableGlossary/#ammunition) 20, [brutal](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/WeaponTableGlossary/#brutal) 1).

**Buckler Bash:** *Melee Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 3 bludgeoning damage.

### Special Abilities
**Nimble Escape:** One time per turn, the goblin can [Hide](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Skills/Stealth/Hide/), or [Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Moves/Step/) as a free action.

### Proficiencies
* *Armour.* Training
* *Weapons.* Blades (Trained), Bows (Trained), Simple Weapons (Trained)
* *Languages.* Goblin (Native) and one other common language (Accented)

### Senses
* Awareness 9 (1d20)
* Low-Light Vision (60 ft.)

# Goblin Boss
*Small Humanoid (Goblinoid), Neutral Evil*

### Defences
**STR:** 10, **DEX:** 12, **CON:** 10, **INT:** 10, **WIS:** 9, **CHA:** 10<br>
+1 [parry](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Armour/Glossary/#parry) bonus vs. melee attacks (Scimitar, Buckler)

**Stamina:** 20 (d6 Recovery Die)

**Damage Reduction:** 4, +1 vs. <img src="https://img.icons8.com/ios-filled/12/FFFFFF/sword.png">, **Coverage:** 5-10 (Chain Shirt, Leather Armour)

**Coverage:** 4, 5-10

### Ability Scores
**STR:** 10 (+0), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 10 (+0), **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 8 (-1), **CHA:** 10 (+0)

**Speed:** 24 feet

### Actions
**Scimitar:** *Melee Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 1d6+2 slashing damage.

**Javelin:** *Ranged Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1 to hit, range 30 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 1d6+2 piercing damage ([sharp 1](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#sharp)).

**Buckler Bash:** *Melee Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 3 bludgeoning damage.

### Special Abilities
**Nimble Escape:** One time per turn, the goblin boss can [Hide](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Skills/Stealth/Hide/), or [Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Moves/Step/) as a free action.

**Redirect Attack:** The first time each round that a creature that the goblin boss can see targets it with an attack, choose another goblin within 5 feet of the goblin boss. The two goblins swap places and the chosen goblin becomes the target instead.

### Proficiencies
* *Armour.* Training
* *Weapons.* Blades (Trained), Bows (Trained), Simple Weapons (Trained)
* *Languages.* Goblin (Native) and one other common language (Accented)

### Senses
* Awareness 9 (1d20)
* Low-Light Vision (60 ft.)
