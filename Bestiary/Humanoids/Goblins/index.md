# Goblin (stat block 1)
*Small Humanoid, Neutral Evil*

### Defences
**STR:** 9, **DEX:** 12, **CON:** 10, **INT:** 10, **WIS:** 9, **CHA:** 9<br>
**Stamina:** 10 (d4 Recovery Die)<br>
**Damage Reduction:** 1 (Leather Armour), +1 DR vs. Slashing damage<br>
**Coverage:** None <br>
The goblin can use the [Parry](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/ArmourandShields/ArmourTableGlossary/#parry) action while equipped with a buckler and/or shortsword

### Ability Scores
**STR:** 8 (-1), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 10 (+0), **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 8 (-1), **CHA:** 8 (-1)<br>
**Speed:** 25 feet

### Equipment
Leather Armour, Buckler, 1 Melee Weapon, 1 Ranged Weapon

### Actions
**- Shortsword:** *Trained Melee Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, range 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d3+2 Piercing/Slashing damage.<br>
**- Shortbow:** *Trained Ranged Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, range 90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d6+2 piercing damage. [Brutal 1](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#brutal)

### Skill Training
Stealth(Trained)

### Special Actions
**Nimble Escape:** Once per turn, when the goblin takes the [Hide](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Momves/Step) or [Stride](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Momves/Step) action, it can first use the [Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Momves/Step) action for free.

### Senses
**Awareness:** 9 (1d20)<br>
**Low-Light Vision (60 ft.)**<br>
**Languages:** Goblin(Fluent), Common(Broken)

# Goblin Boss
*Small Humanoid, Neutral Evil*

### Defences
**STR:** 11, **DEX:** 13, **CON:** 10, **INT:** 10, **WIS:** 9, **CHA:** 10<br>
**Stamina:** 20 (d6 Recovery Die)<br>
**Damage Reduction:** 5 (Chain Shirt+Leather Armour), +1 DR vs. Slashing damage<br>
**Coverage:** 4, 5-10 <br>
The goblin can use the [Parry](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/ArmourandShields/ArmourTableGlossary/#parry) action while equipped with a shortsword

### Ability Scores
**STR:** 10 (+0), **DEX:** 14 (+2), **CON:** 10 (+0), **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 8 (-1), **CHA:** 10 (+0)<br>
**Speed:** 25 feet

### Equipment
Chain Shirt, Chain Coif, Leather Armour, Wooden Shield, 1 Melee Weapon, 1 Ranged Weapon

### Actions
**- Shortsword:** *Trained Melee Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1+2 to hit, range 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d3+2 Piercing/Slashing damage.<br>
**- Javelin:** *Trained Melee/Ranged Weapon Attack,* 2d20kh1 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1d6+2 piercing damage. [Sharp 1](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#sharp)

### Skill Training
Stealth(Trained)

### Special Actions
**Nimble Escape:** Once per turn, when the goblin takes the [Hide](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Momves/Step) or [Stride](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Momves/Step) action, it can first use the [Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Momves/Step) action for free.<br>
**Redirect Attacks:** The first time each round the goblin boss is targeted for an attack by a creature it can see, the target of the attack changes to another creature allied with the goblin boss within 5 feet. This change of target can include another goblin boss and does not redirect to another creature again.

### Senses
**Awareness:** 10 (1d20)<br>
**Low-Light Vision (60 ft.)**<br>
**Languages:** Goblin(Fluent), Common(Broken)
