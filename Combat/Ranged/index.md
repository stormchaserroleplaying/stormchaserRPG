# Ranged Actions
Ranged actions are tactical options specifically for ranged combat.

Ranged combat comes with multiple steps a combatant takes to perform an attack. The skill of using the weapon matches that of a blade duelist, but takes a different form through disciplined steps to keep a weapon firing in steady succession. These actions do not need to be completed with consecutive actions as the flow of combat sometimes necessitates adjusting to unpredictable conditions.

## Draw Ammunition
First, you must take out the ammunition using the [Interact](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Interact) action. Everything needed to load the weapon is included in this action from the projectile itself to propellants or loading tools.

## Load the Weapon
Second, the ranged weapon is loaded with the ammunition. Check the weapon's [Load](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#load) property. The number shown is the amount of actions needed to finish loading 1 shot of ammunition. If the weapon has an [Ammunition](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#ammunition) property with a 2 or higher, that many shots are loaded and can be shot before needing to reload again.

A creature or player spending actions to [Load](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#load) also has the [Flat-Footed](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/FlatFooted) condition. If you take damage while reloading, there is a chance that the interruption will throw you off and hinder your ability to reload. If an enemy attack roll exceeds your weapon skill, refer to the following table for resulting complications.

| Exceeds Weapon Skill By... | Complication |
|:--------------------------:|:------------:|
| 1-3                        | You are thrown off balance, the current Load time increases by 1 |
| 4-6                        | You drop your ammunition and must restart the [Load](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#load) process |
| 7+                         | You drop your ammunition and it becomes fouled. Restart the [Load](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Equipment/Weapons/Glossary/#load) process and lose the ammount of ammunition your weapon would hold after reloading. |

The process of reloading a weapon can be willingly interrupted without penalty. The remaining actions will still need to be spent to fire the weapon again, but damage taken will not affect the reloading process and the [Flat-Footed](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/FlatFooted) condition is removed until reloading resumes.

## Shoot the Weapon
When you launch a projectile, perform the [Strike](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Strike) action, or any of the alternate Ranged actions detailed in this section. If you use the [Aim](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Ranged/Aim) action, you perform the action but do not launch your projectile until you perform a ranged action with an attack roll.
