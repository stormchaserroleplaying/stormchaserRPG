# Illusion

## Evil Eye
*Illusion Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 30 Feet<br>
**Components:** S*<br>
**Duration:** 1 Round
**Prerequisite:** [Evil Eye](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Warlock/#evil-eye)

*Your patron's resentment manifests through your baleful glare.*

Make a spell attack against the target's Wisdom defense. On a hit, they become [Nauseated](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Nauseated/) for the duration of the spell. If the spell is applied a second time, the target becomes [Poisoned](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Poisoned/) for 1 additional round. The target can make a CON check to remove the effect and end the spell.

## Illusory Toils
*Illusion Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 60 Feet<br>
**Components:** S, V*<br>
**Duration:** Instant

Make a spell attack against a target's Wisdom defense. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Psychic damage. On a critical hit, the target gains 1 level of [Exhaustion](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Exhaustion/). This spell cannot put a target above [Exhaustion](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Exhaustion/) level 3.

## Distortion Lens
*Illusion Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 5 square Foot cube within 30 Feet<br>
**Components:** M, S, V *(A small, glass marble.)*<br>
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

You create a clear, intangible, magical lens which hovers midair and distorts space for anyone who looks through it. If an ally's ranged attack passes through it, the attack's range increases by 10 feet. If an enemy's attack passes through it, its range is reduced by 10 feet. An enemy can determine if the range penalty will result in a miss with a DC 10 [Investigation](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Skills/Investigation/) check. The lens can also be used to look around corners, but the effect can be perceived from both sides.

Each time you [Sustain](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Spellcasting/Casting/Duration/#sustaining-spells) the spell, you can choose to move the lens anywhere within 30 feet of you.

## Minor Illusion
*Illusion Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 1 Action<br>
**Range:** 30 Feet<br>
**Components:** S, M *(A bit of fleece.)*<br>
**Duration:** 1 Minute

*You create a small illusory object or sound.*

You create a sound or an image of an object within range that lasts for the duration. The illusion also ends if you dismiss it using an action or cast this spell again.

If you create a sound, its volume can range from a whisper to a scream. It can be your voice, someone else’s voice, a lion’s roar, a beating of drums, or any other sound you choose. The sound continues unabated throughout the duration, or you can make discrete sounds at different times before the spell ends.

If you create an image of an object (such as a chair, muddy footprints, or a small chest) it must be no larger than a 5-foot cube. The image can’t create sound, light, smell, or any other sensory effect. Physical interaction with the image reveals it to be an illusion, because things can pass through it.

If a creature uses its action to examine the sound or image, the creature can determine that it is an illusion with a successful [Investigation](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Skills/Investigation/) check vs your illusion spell skill. If a creature discerns the illusion for what it is, the illusion becomes faint to the creature.
