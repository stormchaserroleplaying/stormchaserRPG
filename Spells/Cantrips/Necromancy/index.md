# Necromancy

## Ancient Dust
*Necromancy Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 15 Foot Cone<br>
**Components:** S, V<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You cough up a cloud of grave soil, leaving spots of corruption to fester on your victims. Make a spell attack roll against the DEX defense of all targets in range. On a hit, the target(s) take Decay damage equal to your spellcasting attribute modifer. On a critical hit, the target also takes 1 [Persistent Decay Damage](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Persistent/) for 1 minute.

## Inside Ropes
*Necromancy Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** Self<br>
**Components:** S, V<br>
**Duration:** 1 Hour

You reach into your body and produce a large handful of innards and viscera which bind together to form a length of cord. This process does not cause harm to the caster. The produced innards function as 50 feet of hempen rope for the duration of the spell. The "rope" can be used for any purpose the same as mundane rope including securing an object, the [Tie a Knot](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Skills/SleightofHand/tieknot/) action, climing, etc. The loose end of your "rope" can be attached to a solid anchor point, thus allowing you to [Climb](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Exploration/Movement/SpecialTypesofMovement/#climbing-swimming-and-crawling) with a base DC of 15. If you critically fail your [Climb](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Exploration/Movement/SpecialTypesofMovement/#climbing-swimming-and-crawling) check, you can make a flat DC 5 check to prevent falling. If the fall is more than 50 feet, your "rope" catches at 50 feet, ending the spell immediately and you fall the rest of the distance with any damage calculated from where the spell ended.

You can end this spell at any time, at which time the innards harmlessly retract into your body. If your organic rope is broken (using the same DR and integrity as hempen rope), the spell ends immediately, the "rope" dissolves or returns to your body, and you are made whole but take 1dR Decay damage. This damage cannot be reduced.

## Lich Slap
*Necromancy Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 120 Feet<br>
**Components:** V, S<br>
**Duration:** 1 Round

*You create a ghostly, skeletal hand that assails a creature with the chill of the grave.*

Make a ranged necromancy attack against a creature that you can see within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 + your spellcasting attribute modifier decay damage and cannot regain Stamina until the start of your next turn. Until then, the hand clings to the target.

If you hit an undead target, it also has -1d on attack rolls against you until the end of your next turn.

## Sapping Sting
*Necromancy Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 1 Action<br>
**Range:** 30 Feet<br>
**Components:** V, S<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous

*You sap the vitality of a creature.*

Make a necromancy attack roll vs the Constitution defence of a target that you can see within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 + your spellcasting attribute modifier profane damage and falls [Prone](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Prone/).

## Toll the Dead
*Necromancy Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 60 Feet<br>
**Components:** S, V<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous

*The sound of dolorous bells around a creature, for a moment.*

You point at one creature that you can see within range and make a necromancy attack roll vs the target's Wisdom defence. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 + your spellcasting attribute modifier profane damage. If the target is missing any of its Stamina, it instead takes 1d12 + your spellcasting attribute modifier profane damage.

## Void Warp
*Necromancy Cantrip*<br>
**Casting Time:** 2 Actions<br>
**Range:** 60 Feet<br>
**Components:** S, V<br>
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Make a spell attack against the target's CON defense. On a hit, they take 1d6 Decay damage. On a critical hit, they are also [Nauseated](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Nauseated/) until the end of their next turn. This spell has no effect on Undead or Constructs.
