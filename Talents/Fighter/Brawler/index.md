# Brawler
Most fighters require weapons. However, brawlers are their own weapon.

## Talents

| Talent | CP | Prerequisites |
|:------:|:--:|:-------------:|
| Moxie                | 5  | Combat Superiority |
| Bloodied But Unbowed | 10  | Combat Superiority |
| Haymaker             | 15 | Bloodied But Unbowed |
| Moxie Fuelled Fists  | 10 | Moxie |
| Down But Not Out     | 30 | Haymaker |
| Unbreakable          | 65 | Down But Not Out |
| Fighting Spirit      | 80 | Unbreakable |

### Moxie

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Combat Superiority
*Your experience laying the beatdown on others has given you moxie that you can channel in the midst of battle.*

You can expend [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana) to fuel the following moxie abilities:

#### Brace Up

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

##### **Actions:** 1
You can spend 1 [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana) to brace for attacks. You gain temporary stamina equal to 1dR + your constitution modifier.

#### The Old One-Two

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

##### **Actions:** Free
Immediately after you take the [Strike](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Strike/) action, you may spend 1 [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana) to make an unarmed melee attack as a free action. This attack is subject to the multiattack penalty.

#### Stick and Move

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

##### **Actions:** Free
You can spend 1 [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana) to perform a [Fight Dirty](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Melee/Dirty/), [Grapple](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Melee/Grapple/), [Shove](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Melee/Shove/), or [Stride](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Moves/Stride/) action as a free action. Any attack made is subject to the multiattack penalty.

### Bloodied But Unbowed

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 10 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Combat Superiority<br>Actions: Free
If, over the course of the last round, you took damage that reduced you to half your maximum stamina or less, then, as a free action at the start of your turn, you may choose to gain temporary stamina equal to 1dR + your constitution modifier. Additionally, you regain all expended [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana).

You cannot use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Haymaker

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 15 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Bloodied But Unbowed
Before you make a [Strike](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Strike/) with an unarmed or improvised weapon, you can declare that you are swinging wild haymakers. You make all attack rolls until the end of this turn with -1d. However, when you deal damage with an improvised weapon or unarmed [Strike](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Strike/), you do not roll the weapon’s damage die, but instead use the maximum die result.

### Moxie Fuelled Fists

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 10 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Moxie
Your Unarmed [Strike](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Strike/)s count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to non-magical attacks and damage.

### Down But Not Out

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 30 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Haymaker
When you use your Bloodied But Unbowed talent, you can choose to add 1dM to your damage with unarmed attacks and improvised weapons for the next minute. 

You can use this feature again after you complete a long rest.

### Unbreakable

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 65 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Down But Not Out
Whenever you are hit by an attack, you can spend 1 [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana) to make it miss instead.

### Fighting Spirit

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 80 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Unbreakable
When you are reduced to 0 Stamina, you can choose to regain half of your maximum Stamina and half of your maximum [mana](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Creation/Attributes/Other/#mana). However, you also gain a level of exhaustion.

You cannot use this feature again until you finish a long rest.
