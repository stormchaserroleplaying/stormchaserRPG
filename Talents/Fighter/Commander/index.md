# Commander
Commanders inspire greatness in others by committing brave deeds in battle. The mere presence of a commander in a hamlet is enough to cause some orcs and bandits to seek easier prey. A lone commander is a skilled warrior, but a commander leading a band of allies can transform even the most poorly equipped militia into a ferocious war band.

### Rallying Cry

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** [Combat Superiority](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#combat-superiority)<br>**Actions:** 1
*You learn how to inspire your allies to fight on past their injuries.*

Choose a number of allied creatures that can see or hear you equal to your charisma modifier. Each creature gains temporary stamina equal to 1dM + your charisma modifier.

Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest before you can use it again.

### Inspiring Surge

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 25 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Rallying Cry and [Action Surge](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#action-surge)
When you use your [Action Surge](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#action-surge) feature, you can instead choose an allied creature that can see and hear you to benefit from the feature on its next turn.

If your [Action Surge](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#action-surge) feature grants more than one additional action, then you may divide these amongst a number of allied creatures that can see and hear you equal to your charisma modifier.

### Bulwark

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 30 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Inspiring Surge and [Indomitable](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#indomitable)
If you have not used your [Indomitable](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#indomitable) feature since your last rest, then you may extend it to an ally that can see and hear you. The next attack to hit that ally must be rerolled, taking the lower result.
