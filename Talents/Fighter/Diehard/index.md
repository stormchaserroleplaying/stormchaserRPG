# Diehard
The Diehard is a fighter who draws on an implacable fighting spirit to overcome enemies. A Diehard’s resolve is nearly unbreakable and the enemies in their path have two choices: yield or die fighting.

## Talents

| Talent                | CP | Prerequisites |
|:---------------------:|:--:|:-------------:|
| Fighting Spirit       | 15 | [Combat Superiority](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#combat-superiority) |
| Tireless Spirit       | 25 | Fighting Spirit |
| Strength Before Death | 30 | Tireless Spirit |

### Fighting Spirit

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 15 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** [Combat Superiority](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Fighter/#combat-superiority)<br>**Actions:** 1
*Your intensity in battle can shield you and help you strike true.*

You gain +1d on weapon attack rolls until the end of the current turn. When you do so, you also gain 1dR temporary stamina.

Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest before you can use it again.

### Tireless Spirit

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 25 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Fighting Spirit
The amount of temporary stamina granted by your Fighting Spirit increases to 2dR.

Additionally, you can now use your Fighting Spirit feature again after finishing a [breather](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Exploration/Resting/Breather/).

### Strength Before Death

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 30 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Rapid Strike
*Your fighting spirit can delay the grasp of death to protect your honour.*

The amount of temporary stamina granted by your Fighting Spirit increases to 3dR.

Additionally, when you take damage that reduces you to 0 stamina and doesn't kill you outright, you can delay falling [unconscious](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Unconscious). You immediately interrupt the current turn to take an extra turn.

You have 0 stamina during this extra turn, taking damage causes death saving throw failures as normal and three death saving throw failures will kill you. When the extra turn ends, you fall [unconscious](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Unconscious) if you still have 0 hit points.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
