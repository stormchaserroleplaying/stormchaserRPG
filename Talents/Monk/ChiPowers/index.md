# Chi Powers

| Chi Power         | CP | Prerequisites |
|:-----------------:|:--:|:-------------:|
| Dedicated Weapon  | 1  | None          |
| Deflect Missiles  | 5  | None          |
| Flurry of Blows   | 1  | None          |
| Focused Aim       | 5  | None          |
| Patient Defense   | 1  | None          |
| Quickened Healing | 2  | None          |
| Step of the Wind  | 1  | None          |

## Dedicated Weapon

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 1 Character Point
Whenever you finish a short or long rest, you can touch one weapon and focus on it. That weapon is then considered a monk weapon until you use this feature again.

The chosen weapon must meet these criteria:
* You must be trained with it.
* It may not have the two-handed and unwieldy properties.

## Deflect Missiles

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points
If you catch a missile that was shot at you, you can spend 1 chi point to immediately make a ranged attack as a free action with the weapon or piece of ammunition you just caught. The missile counts as a monk weapon for the attack, which has a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet.

## Flurry of Blows

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 1 Character Points<br>**Mana Cost:** 1 Point
When you take the [Strike](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Strike/) action, you may spend 1 mana to ignore the [multiple attack penalty](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/General/Specific/Attack/#multiple-attack-penalty).

## Focussed Aim

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Mana Cost:** 1 Point
On your turn, you can spend 1 mana to add +1d to any attack roll that you make.

## Patient Defense

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 1 Character Points<br>**Mana Cost:** 1 Point
You can spend 1 mana to [Dodge](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Dodge/) using only a single action.

## Quickened Healing

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 2 Character Points<br>**Mana Cost:** Varies
You learn the [Heal](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Spells/1/Restoration/#heal) spell. You always have this spell prepared and it does not count against your number of spells prepared. If you do not already have a spellcasting talent, then wisdom is considered to be your spellcasting attribute for the purposes of casting this spell.

You cannot take this talent if you already know this spell (e.g. from [Celestial Legacy](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Species/Lineages/Celestial/#celestial-legacy), or being a [Divine Soul](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Talents/Sorcerer/Divine/#good)).

## Step of the Wind

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 1 Character Points<br>**Mana Cost:** 1 Point
You can spend 1 mana to [Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Step/) or [Stride](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Combat/Actions/Stride/) as a free action on your turn. Additionally, your jump distance is doubled for that turn.
