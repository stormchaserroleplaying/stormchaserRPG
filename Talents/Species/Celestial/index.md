# Celestial Talents

## Celestial Legacy

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Celestial Lineage Phenotype Talent
*Divine blood flows bright in your veins.*

Your phenotype ability improves:
* **Protector:** When you use your Radiant Soul to fly, you do not provoke Attacks of Opportunity when you move. Additionally, your flying speed increases to double your movement speed.
* **Scourge:** When you use your Radiant Consumption to deal extra sacred damage to a target, you can make a trained Charisma attack vs the target's Constitution defence. On a hit, the creature is stunned until the end of your next turn. You can use this ability a number of times per long rest equal to your Charisma modifier.
* **Fallen:** When you use your Necrotic Shroud to frighten creatures within 10 feet of you, they instead become panicked.
* **Herald:** When you use your Radiant Mobility to enhance your speed, you do not provoke Attacls of Opportunity when you move and you can walk on liquids and vertical surfaces.

## Necrotic Shroud

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Fallen Celestial Lineage Phenotype<br>**Actions:** 1
*You unleash the divine energy within yourself, causing your eyes to turn into pools of darkness and two skeletal, ghostly, flightless wings to sprout from your back.*

The instant that you transform, make an innate attack roll vs the Charisma defence of all other creatures within 10 feet of you that can see you. On a hit, they become frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Your transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you end it as an action. During it, once on each of your turns, you can deal extra decay damage to one target when you deal damage to it with an attack or a spell. The extra decay damage is equal to your Constitution modifier.

Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Radiant Consumption

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Scourge Celestial Lineage Phenotype<br>**Actions:** 1
*You unleash the divine energy within yourself, causing a searing light to radiate from you, pouring out of your eyes and mouth and threatening to char you.*

Your transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you end it as an action. During it, you shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. At the end of each of your turns, you and each creature within 10 feet of you take sacred damage equal to your Charisma modifier. Additionally, once on each of your turns, you can deal extra sacred damage to one target when you deal damage to it with an attack or a spell. The extra sacred damage equals your Charisma modifier.

Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Radiant Mobility

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Herald Celestial Lineage Phenotype<br>**Actions:** 1
*You unleash the divine energy within yourself, causing your eyes to glimmer and your feet to glide on waves of divine light.*

Your transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you end it as an action. During it, you double your speed. Once on each of your turns, you can deal extra sacred damage to one target when you deal damage to it with an attack or a spell. The extra radiant damage equals your Charisma modifier.

Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Radiant Soul

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Protector Celestial Lineage Phenotype<br>**Actions:** 1
*You unleash the divine energy within yourself, causing your eyes to glimmer and two luminous, incorporeal wings to sprout from your back.*

Your transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you end it as an action. During it, you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed. Once on each of your turns, you can deal extra sacred damage to one target when you deal damage to it with an attack or a spell. The extra radiant damage equals your Charisma modifier.

Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Anointed Metabolism

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Herald Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Radiant Mobility<br>**Actions:** 1
*You may access your divine speed to augment your internal systems and better neutralize poisons and maladies within your body.*

For the duration, you gain +1d to your Constitution Defense to resist poison and diseases for 1 hour.  

Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

## Desecrated Wings

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Fallen Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Necrotic Shroud
*You force your skeletal wings to manifest physically upon activation of your Necrotic Shroud.*

Your wings now act as natural weapons that increase your reach by 5 feet, inflicting 1d4 plus your charisma modifier in piercing damage. This damage die scales with your unarmed proficiency, (e.g. Untrained is 1d4, Trained is 1d6, Expertise is 1d8, Mastery is 1d10.)

## Heavenly Inferno

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Scourge Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Radiant Consumption <br>**Actions:** 1
*You dramatically intensify the heat produced by your transformation, such that it further sears those you come into contact with.*

The damage you deal at the end of each of your turns can either be sacred or fire damage, and all successful attempts to grapple you cause fire damage to the grappler equal to your Charisma modifier. This damage is taken at the end of each round that the grapple is maintained. Lastly, the light that you shed during this transformation counts as Daylight. 

## Into the Light

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 5 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Protector Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Radiant Soul<br>**Actions:** 1
*During your transformation, you may choose to emit a great, cleansing light from your wings.*

You emit bright light in a 15 foot radius around you and dim light for another 15 feet. This light reveals the true form of shapeshifters, alleviates the Blinded, Deafened, and Panicked conditions, and is capable of piercing magical darkness. Upon contact with such darkness, make a Charisma Defense roll against a DC set by the DM. On a success, the magical darkness is dispelled. 

You must spend 1 action on subsequent turns to maintain this light, as though you were sustaining a spell. 

## Angelic Intonation

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 10 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Protector Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Radiant Soul<br>**Actions:** 2
*During your transformation, you emit the mellifluous tones of life itself, empowering those around you.*

You can intone (singing, speaking, etc.) while your divine transformation is active, empowering a number of targets of your choice equal to your Charisma modifier within a 10 foot radius of you. These targets receive +1d to all Defenses, so long as they stay within this radius. 

You must spend 1 action on subsequent turns to continue your vocalizations and maintain this effect.

## Quickstep Barrage

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 10 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Herald Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Radiant Mobility<br>**Actions:** 3
*You utilize your great speed to buffet an enemy with a rapid series of attacks.*

While within melee range, you strike your target three times, moving quickly enough to attack them from a different direction each time. Each movement during this action incurs an Attack of Opportunity from your target, while each strike after the first benefits from Flanking. You cannot make these attacks with weapons that have the Heavy or Unwieldy properties.

You may use this trait a number of times equal to your Dexterity modifier.

## Luminous Shield

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 10 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Scourge Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Radiant Consumption
*The field of sacred energy exuded by your transformation is powerful enough to disrupt the trajectory of projectiles upon reaching it.*

While your divine transformation is active, you gain +1d to your defenses against ranged attacks and the area in a 10 foot radius around you is considered Difficult Terrain.

## Woeful Hunger

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 10 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Fallen Celestial Lineage Phenotype and Necrotic Shroud
*You can cause one of your strikes to absorb the vitality that your target loses, channeling it into strength.*

Upon successfully damaging a target with an Unarmed attack, you gain temporary stamina equal to 1dR. You may use this trait a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier per Long Rest.
