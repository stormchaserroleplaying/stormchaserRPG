# Fey Talents

## Greater Fey Magic

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** See Table<br>**Prerequisite:** See Table
You learn one or more of the following spells. These spells are always prepared and do not count towards your number of spells known or prepared. You may take this talent multiple times.

| Spell                                                                                            | Cost  | Prerequisite     |
|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|:-----:|:----------------:|
| [Misty Step]()                                                                                   | 4 CP  | 2nd Level Spells |
| [Dispel Magic](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Spells/3/Warding/#dispel-magic) | 6 CP  | 3rd Level Spells |
| [Dimension Door]()                                                                               | 8 CP  | 4th Level Spells |
| [Mislead]()                                                                                      | 10 CP | 5th Level Spells |

## Beguiling Twist

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 25 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** [Fey Lineage](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Species/Lineages/Fey)
The magic of the Feywild guards your mind. You have +1d to your defences vs being [Charmed](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Charmed/) or [Frightened](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Frightened/).

In addition, if you or a creature that you can see within 120 feet of you resisted being [Charmed](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Charmed/) or [Frightened](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Frightened/) since your last turn, then, as an action, you can make a charms attack vs the Wisdom defence of a different creature that you can see within 120 feet of you. On a hit, the target is [Charmed](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Charmed/) or [Frightened](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Conditions/Frightened/) by you (your choice) for 1 minute. The target can make a Wisdom defence check vs your charms skill at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself if successful.

## Fey Reinforcements

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 35 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Beguiling Twist
The royal courts of the Feywild have blessed you with the assistance of fey beings: you know the [Summon Fey](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Spells/3/Conjuration/#summon-fey) spell. It doesn't count against the number of spells that you know and you can cast it without a material component. You can also cast it once without spending mana, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest.

Whenever you start casting the spell, you can modify it so that it doesn't have to be sustained. If you do so, the spell's duration becomes 1 minute for that casting.

## Misty Wanderer

<div style="margin-top:-10px;"></div>

#### **Cost:** 65 Character Points<br>**Prerequisite:** Fey Reinforcements
You can slip in and out of the Feywild to move in a blink of an eye: you can cast [Misty Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Spells/2/Conjuration/#misty-step) without expending mana. You can do so a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

In addition, whenever you cast [Misty Step](https://stormchaserroleplaying.com/stormchaserRPG/Spells/2/Conjuration/#misty-step), you can bring along one willing creature you can see within 5 feet of you. That creature teleports to an unoccupied space of your choice within 5 feet of your destination space.
